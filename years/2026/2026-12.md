## How to use this page

- Work through the weeks in order; each checkbox is a small, achievable action.
- Aim for **3–4 hours per week**, but move tasks around exams.
- For labs, use **only authorized training environments**; for analytics use synthetic or appropriately licensed public data.
- Never commit passwords, personal records, tokens or confidential logs.
- At the end of each week, write 2–4 sentences in a comment on the [monthly GitHub Issue](../../issues): what I learned, what I tried, what confused me, and a safe link to evidence.
- **Mentor review:** check the result at the end of the month; do not close the Issue before review.
