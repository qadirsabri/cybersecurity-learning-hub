# May 2029 — Certification preparation

**Monthly goal:** Choose SC-900 or Security+ based on readiness and budget

## Week 1

- [ ] **Learn/do:** Read the current official certification objectives and list topics that are unfamiliar.
- [ ] **Try it:** spend 20–30 minutes on a tiny practical exercise or safe training lab.
- [ ] **Show your work:** write 2–4 sentences: what I learned, what I tried, what confused me, and where the evidence is saved.

## Week 2

- [ ] **Learn/do:** Study two small objective areas; write plain-language explanations and examples.
- [ ] **Try it:** spend 20–30 minutes on a tiny practical exercise or safe training lab.
- [ ] **Show your work:** write 2–4 sentences: what I learned, what I tried, what confused me, and where the evidence is saved.

## Week 3

- [ ] **Learn/do:** Answer practice questions, review mistakes and note gaps without memorizing answers.
- [ ] **Try it:** spend 20–30 minutes on a tiny practical exercise or safe training lab.
- [ ] **Show your work:** write 2–4 sentences: what I learned, what I tried, what confused me, and where the evidence is saved.

## Week 4

- [ ] **Learn/do:** Review readiness and cost. Record a realistic decision or next study plan; taking an exam is optional.
- [ ] **Try it:** spend 20–30 minutes on a tiny practical exercise or safe training lab.
- [ ] **Show your work:** write 2–4 sentences: what I learned, what I tried, what confused me, and where the evidence is saved.

## Weekly routine

Each week: approximately **1 hour learning**, **1.5 hours guided practice** and **up to 1 hour notes or code**. Shorter weeks are fine during university exams. Do not move on until you can explain the basic idea.

## End-of-month review
- [ ] Four weekly notes (or a documented exam-related adjustment)
- [ ] Safe example, code, screenshot or written artifact
- [ ] Progress comment added to the [monthly GitHub Issues](../../issues)
- [ ] Mentor feedback reviewed

**Safety:** Use authorized labs and synthetic or licensed public data only. Never publish credentials, private logs or personal information.
